# Printer not working: quick fixes

**Applies to:** office network printers
**Keywords:** printer, printing, print queue, paper jam, offline, toner

## Symptoms
- Your document doesn't print, or gets stuck "in queue"
- The printer shows **Offline**
- Paper jams, blank pages, or faded prints

## Steps
1. **Check the printer itself.** Is it on? Look at the display for paper jam, out-of-paper, or low-toner messages and follow the on-screen instructions.
2. **Pick the right printer.** In the print dialog, confirm the printer name matches the one on the device label.
3. **Clear stuck jobs.** In Windows: *Settings → Bluetooth & devices → Printers & scanners →* select the printer *→ Open print queue →* cancel all jobs. Then print again.
4. **Restart.** Turn the printer off for 30 seconds, then on. Restart your computer too.
5. **Check the connection.** You must be on the **office network** (or connected to VPN) to reach network printers.
6. **Paper jam:** open only the doors the display points to, remove paper gently in the direction it feeds, and never use tools.

## Verify it worked
- A one-page test print comes out clean and complete.
- The printer shows **Ready**, not Offline.

## When to contact IT
Submit **Fix a problem → Report a hardware problem** if:
- the printer still shows Offline for everyone, or shows an error code
- it needs toner or parts
- there's a **burning smell, smoke, or sparks**. Turn it off at the switch, don't use it, and **mark the request High**.

Include the printer name or asset tag in **Affected hardware**.
