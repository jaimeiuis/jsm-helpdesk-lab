# Computer running slow: what to try first

**Applies to:** company laptops and desktops
**Keywords:** slow, freezing, lag, not responding, high CPU, performance, frozen

## Symptoms
- Apps take a long time to open or show "Not responding"
- The fan is loud and the computer feels hot
- Typing or the mouse lags behind

## Steps
1. **Restart.** Use *Start → Power → Restart*, not just closing the lid. Many slowdowns come from pending updates or days without a restart.
2. **Close what you aren't using.** Browser tabs and video calls use a lot of memory.
3. **Check Task Manager.** Press **Ctrl + Shift + Esc** → **Processes** → sort by **CPU** or **Memory**. Close apps you recognize and don't need. **Don't end processes you don't recognize.**
4. **Free up disk space.** *Settings → System → Storage.* Keep at least 15% free. Empty the Recycle Bin and move large personal files to your approved cloud storage.
5. **Let updates finish.** *Settings → Windows Update.* Install pending updates and restart.
6. **Laptops:** plug into power. Battery-saver mode slows the computer down on purpose.

## Verify it worked
- Apps open in a few seconds, and Task Manager shows CPU usage mostly under 50% when idle.

## When to contact IT
Submit **Fix a problem → Report a hardware problem** if:
- it's still slow after a restart and updates
- you see **pop-ups, unknown programs, or a changed browser homepage**. That could be malware: **stop using the computer, disconnect from Wi-Fi, and mark the request High.**
- the disk is full and you can't free up space

Include your computer name in **Affected hardware**. Find it under *Settings → System → About*.
