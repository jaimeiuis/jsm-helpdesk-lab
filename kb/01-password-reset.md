# How to reset your password or unlock your account

**Applies to:** your company account (email, Wi-Fi, VPN, shared drives)
**Keywords:** password, locked out, forgot password, account locked, MFA, sign in

## Symptoms
- "Your account has been locked" or "Incorrect password" when you sign in
- You forgot your password, or it expired
- Your MFA (authenticator app) codes are rejected

## Before you start
- **IT will never ask for your password.** Don't share it in a ticket, chat, or email.
- Accounts lock after several wrong attempts. **Wait 15 minutes** before trying again; the lock clears automatically.

## Steps
1. Check **Caps Lock** and your keyboard language, then try again **once**.
2. If you forgot your password, use **"Forgot password?"** on the sign-in page and follow the email or text prompts.
3. Choose a new password: at least 12 characters, not reused, not your name or the company name. A short passphrase works well (for example, three random words).
4. **Update the password on your other devices** (phone email, Wi-Fi, VPN). Old saved passwords re-lock your account.
5. **MFA problems:** check that your phone's date and time are set to automatic, then use a fresh code. Codes expire after about 30 seconds.

## Verify it worked
- You can sign in to email and open a shared drive with the new password.
- Your phone's email syncs without asking for a password again.

## When to contact IT
Submit **Accounts & access → Account access or password help** if:
- you're still locked out after waiting 15 minutes
- you don't receive the reset email or text
- you got a new phone and lost your authenticator app
- you suspect someone else knows your password (**mark it High**)
