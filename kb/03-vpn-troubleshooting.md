# VPN won't connect: troubleshooting

**Applies to:** remote access with the company VPN client
**Keywords:** VPN, remote access, can't connect, work from home, connection failed

## Symptoms
- The VPN client says "Connection failed" or keeps reconnecting
- You're connected, but can't reach shared drives or internal sites
- The connection is very slow

## Steps
1. **Check your internet first.** Can you open a public website? If not, restart your home router and reconnect to Wi-Fi.
2. **Sign in again.** Disconnect, quit the VPN client completely, reopen it, and sign in. Approve the MFA prompt on your phone.
3. **Password changed recently?** Use your **new** password in the VPN client (see *How to reset your password*).
4. **Restart your computer.** This clears most stuck network adapters.
5. **Public or hotel Wi-Fi:** open a browser first and accept the Wi-Fi terms, *then* connect the VPN.
6. **Slow connection:** close large downloads and video calls you don't need, or move closer to your router.

## Verify it worked
- The VPN client shows **Connected**.
- You can open a shared drive or an internal site.

## When to contact IT
Submit **Fix a problem → Report a software problem** if:
- the VPN fails after all of the steps above
- you see a certificate or "not authorized" error. **Include a screenshot.**
- **everyone** on your team can't connect. That's an outage, so **mark it High**.
